PRIVACY POLICY · VERSO

Privacy.

LAST UPDATED 16 OCTOBER 2026

Verso stores everything on your device. There are no accounts, we run no servers that hold your data, and we never see your cards, your name, or your usage. Your information stays with you.

What we store, and where

Your profile name, the cards you select, and your preferences are stored locally on your device (in browser/app storage). This data is never transmitted to us and we have no access to it. Deleting the app or clearing its data permanently erases it.

No account, no tracking

Verso requires no sign-up, email, or login. We do not collect personal data, do not track you across sites, and do not sell or share any data — because we never receive it.

Optional Google Places feature

If you choose to enable smart store detection by adding your own Google Places API key, the merchant or place name you type is sent to Google to identify its category, governed by Google's Privacy Policy (policies.google.com/privacy). This feature is off by default and only runs if you enable it. We do not receive or store these queries.

Children

Verso is not directed at anyone under 18.

Your rights under the PDPA

As all data is stored on your own device, you remain in full control of it at all times and can erase it via the app's Delete All Data function. This policy is provided in line with Singapore's Personal Data Protection Act (PDPA).

Changes

We may update this policy; material changes will be reflected by the "Last updated" date above.

Contact & your data

Verso stores no personal data on any server, so there is nothing for us to access, correct, or delete on your behalf. You control all your data directly and can erase it at any time using the Delete All Data button in the app.
